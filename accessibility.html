Hospitality on the page

Accessibility.

Accessibility is part of hospitality. We want everyone to be able to read, cook, and join the conversation.

What we do

  • Semantic headings, landmarks, and a “skip to main content” link on every page.
  • Visible keyboard focus and touch targets of at least 44 pixels.
  • Result counts, saved states, and form errors are announced to screen readers.
  • Form errors are written beside each field and never shown by colour alone.
  • A light and a dark theme, with text contrast checked in both.
  • Animations are reduced when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Every recipe can be printed without the navigation.

Tell us what isn’t working

If something gets in your way, please let us know. We read every note.
