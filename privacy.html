The small print

Your privacy.

We collect as little as we can and only use it for the reason you gave it to us.

What we collect

When you send us a note, we receive your name, email address, optional country, and your message. When you subscribe to our letter, we receive your email address.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message.
  • To send our monthly letter, if you asked for it. Every letter includes a way to unsubscribe.

What stays on your device

Your saved recipes and your light or dark theme choice are stored in your own browser (local storage). They are never sent to us, and you can clear them at any time from your browser settings.

Questions

If you have a question about your information, send us a note.
